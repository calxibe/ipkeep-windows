IPKeep for Windows - unsigned beta

This preview is for beta testing. It is not digitally signed. Windows may display an unknown publisher or SmartScreen warning.

Setup installs IPKeep for all users on this computer. Your existing settings, encrypted tokens, hostnames, and activity logs are preserved.

After a new installation, open IPKeep, add your token, choose your hostnames, and enable updates. An existing background service is updated automatically; a paused service stays paused.

Uninstalling stops and removes the IPKeep service and application files. Local settings, tokens, and logs are retained so that you can reinstall. Your online account and hostnames are not deleted.

Automatic application updates are not included. Install a newer beta manually when one is available.
